## Project overview goals
[Clawed by Kamo] 
The business was established in early 2026 after a long time of watching nail videos on social media. I finally got the courage to register for beginner nail training, to learn the basics of being a nail tech. What was just admiration grew to doing friends and family's nails in to a small nail shop that is growing in to something big. Every French tip, cuticle care,  every selfie after-appointment reminds us why we love what we do. 

## Website objective
•	Drive online bookings 
•	Showcase work 
•	Boost local SEO 
•	Build community 

## Key features and functinality
•	Homepage, first impression + Book Now call to action. 
•	Services + pricing, clear menu: acrylics, gel, pedicures, kiddies, add-ons. No "DM for price" 
•	Online gallery: before and after photos + video reels, builds trust instantly. 
•	About us: Your story, team photos, hygiene standards. People buy from people. 
•	Reviews + testimonials: Google reviews + clients photos embedded. 
Functionality 
•	Easy booking 
•	Showcase 
•	Trust: reviews, hygiene, and location with google maps 
•	Growth: SEO, promotions and Instagram reels.  
•	Convenience: Loyalty cards and gift cards.
