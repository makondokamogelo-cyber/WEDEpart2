GET TO KNOW US

About Clawed by Kamo

Clawed by Kamo is a nail beauty business dedicated to helping clients express themselves through beautiful, creative and professionally styled nails.

We believe that nails are more than just part of a beauty routine. They are a way of showing personality, confidence and individual style.

Our Story

Clawed by Kamo was created from a passion for beauty, creativity and nail art.

Our aim is to create a welcoming nail experience where clients can explore different colours, shapes and designs while receiving friendly and professional service.

From simple everyday nails to creative designs for special occasions, we want every client to leave feeling beautiful and confident.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide beautiful, creative and affordable nail services while creating a comfortable and friendly experience for every client.

Our Vision

Our vision is to grow Clawed by Kamo into a trusted nail beauty brand known for creativity, quality, professionalism and excellent customer service.
